What is Game-Hub?

Game-Hub is a lightweight online community built around simple games. Right now, the community focuses on one experience: private two-player online chess.

No account is required. Pick a player name, create a room, share the six-character code, and play with your friend.

How a game works

  1. Enter your player name.
  2. Create a room and send the room code to your opponent.
  3. Your opponent enters their name and joins the code.
  4. White moves first. The two players play in real time.
  5. When the game ends, either player can start a new game in the same room.
