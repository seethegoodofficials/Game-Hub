About this website

Game-Hub is a completely free gaming community where simple and group games are available. Play with friends in real time — create a room, share a short invite code, and start playing instantly.

Currently featured:

  • Online Chess – Real-time multiplayer chess with room codes

More simple and multiplayer games will be added over time. The goal is a lightweight place where anyone can jump in and play with friends without accounts or complicated setup.

How it works

  • Create a private room → get a 6-character code
  • Share the code with your friend(s)
  • They join and the game starts automatically
  • No registration required
